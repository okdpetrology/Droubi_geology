NSF Doom Scroller!!!

Scroll through the last few years of NSF-GEO awards.

This project began in 2023 as an experiement on how to improve communication of the public-facing NSF-GEO awards database AND for me to be able to quickly scroll through and key word search awards on my phone. It has been revived as of Nov 2026 for testing purposes. Though probably very easy to do... none of this was done using AI tools.
